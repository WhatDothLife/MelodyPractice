ExperimentalTRYING OUT

Each bar picks one of the selected modes and leans into its sound: the tonic is favored, most of all on beats 1 and 3, the mode's color tone is brought out (B in Dorian and Lydian, F in Phrygian, Mixolydian and Aeolian, F and B in Ionian), and C is played down so the bar does not slide back into plain C major. Half the bars start right on the tonic. Chord-tone moves outline the mode's home chord. A bar only carries a mode name if its tonic lands on beat 1 or 3 or opens or closes the bar, and, when the bar has four or more notes, its color tone sounds too.
